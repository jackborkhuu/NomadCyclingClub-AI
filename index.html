About

Nomad Cycling Club is a community founded by Mongolian riders in Washington State. We ride to build connection, celebrate culture, and keep exploring together.

Mission

Our mission is to build an inclusive cycling community that brings together riders of all backgrounds while honoring our Mongolian roots.

Values

  • Community & Belonging - We ride together and support one another.
  • Cultural Pride - We honor and share our Mongolian heritage.
  • Inclusivity - Riders of all levels have a place here.
  • Wellness & Growth - Cycling builds physical and mental strength.
  • Adventure & Exploration - The ride is part of our identity.
  • Respect & Responsibility - We care for each other and the places we ride.
