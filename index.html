Just now

Welcome to Nomad Cycling Club! We are a team of passionate cyclists and organizers united by a shared love for riding, culture, and community.

Founded by Mongolian cyclists in Washington State, we're powered by volunteers who believe in building a strong and supportive space for everyone—whether you're on your first ride or your hundredth.

Nomad Cycling Club - USA

Our Mission

At Nomad Cycling Club, our mission is to build an inclusive cycling community that brings together riders of all backgrounds while honoring our Mongolian roots.

We aim to inspire wellness, cultural pride, and a spirit of exploration through group rides, events, and shared experiences.

Our Values

Our values guide every ride we take and every relationship we build:

  • Community & Belonging - We ride together and support one another
  • Cultural Pride - We honor and share our Mongolian heritage
  • Inclusivity - Our doors are open to all who ride with heart
  • Wellness & Growth - Cycling is a path to physical and mental strength
  • Adventure & Exploration - Inspired by Mongolia's nomadic legacy
  • Respect & Responsibility - We treat our team, trails, and communities with care

Our values

Community & belonging, cultural pride, inclusivity, wellness & growth, adventure & exploration, respect & responsibility. We ride together and support one another.

Cultural Pride

We honor and share our Mongolian heritage through meaningful traditions, events, and the nomadic spirit that defines who we are. Nomad Cycling Club is powered by volunteers who believe in building a strong and supportive space for everyone.

Who we welcome

  • Beginners starting their cycling journey
  • Experienced riders seeking community
  • People of all backgrounds and skill levels
  • Those who ride for fitness, connection, or adventure
