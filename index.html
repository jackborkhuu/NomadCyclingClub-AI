We are a team of Mongolian bike riders

Nomad Cycling Club

A community founded by Mongolian riders and based in Washington State. We welcome cyclists of all backgrounds and skill levels. At Nomad, we ride with pride, purpose, and the spirit of the open road.

Join us Learn more

Our Mission

Build an inclusive cycling community that brings together riders of all backgrounds while honoring our Mongolian roots. We aim to inspire wellness, cultural pride, and a spirit of exploration through group rides, events, and shared experiences.

By the numbers

  • 50+ Members across Washington State
  • 80+ Group rides per year
  • 10+ Events celebrating culture & community

Our values

Community & belonging, cultural pride, inclusivity, wellness & growth, adventure & exploration, respect & responsibility. We ride together and support one another.

Cultural Pride

We honor and share our Mongolian heritage through meaningful traditions, events, and the nomadic spirit that defines who we are. Nomad Cycling Club is powered by volunteers who believe in building a strong and supportive space for everyone.

Who we welcome

  • Beginners starting their cycling journey
  • Experienced riders seeking community
  • People of all backgrounds and skill levels
  • Those who ride for fitness, connection, or adventure
