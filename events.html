Rides & Events

Nomad Cycling Club hosts 80+ group rides per year plus 10+ cultural and community events. We offer a variety of rides and events throughout Washington State, ranging from casual group rides to challenging routes, so everyone can find their place.

Group Rides

We offer regular group rides throughout the year for all skill levels—beginners to experienced riders. Whether you're riding to explore, train, or connect with others, we have something for you.

Cultural Events

We celebrate our Mongolian heritage through meaningful cultural events and traditions that bring our community together on and off the bike.

Community Rides

Join us for rides that focus on connection, wellness, and exploring Washington State's beautiful routes together as a supportive community.
