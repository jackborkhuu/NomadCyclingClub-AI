Join the Club

If you love cycling and want to ride with a friendly local group, we’d love to have you.

Membership benefits

  • Weekly rides and structured training
  • Access to club route guides and safety information
  • Community events and social rides
  • Support from experienced riders

How to join

  1. Fill out the short interest form on our Contact page.
  2. Attend a welcome ride and meet the group.
  3. Choose the membership level that fits your needs.

Membership fee: $25 per year (nonprofit club support)
