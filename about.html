About Nomad Cycling Club

We are a team of passionate cyclists and organizers united by a shared love for riding, culture, and community. Founded by Mongolian cyclists in Washington State, we're powered by volunteers who believe in building a strong and supportive space for everyone—whether you're on your first ride or your hundredth.

Our story

Nomad Cycling Club was founded by Mongolian riders who wanted to create a space where cycling and cultural pride could come together. What started as a group of passionate cyclists has grown into a vibrant community of 50+ members across Washington State, hosting 80+ group rides per year and 10+ cultural and community events.

Our values

  • Community & Belonging - We ride together and support one another no matter your background, experience level, or bike.
  • Cultural Pride - We honor and share our Mongolian heritage through meaningful traditions and events.
  • Inclusivity - Our doors (and routes) are open to all who ride with heart.
  • Wellness & Growth - Cycling is a path to physical and mental strength.
  • Adventure & Exploration - Inspired by Mongolia's nomadic legacy, we seek the unknown and embrace the journey.
  • Respect & Responsibility - We treat our team, trails, and communities with care.
