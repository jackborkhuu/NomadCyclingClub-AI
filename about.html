About the Club

River City Cycling Club is a nonprofit community cycling group that brings riders together for safe, fun, and supportive rides.

Our story

Founded by local cyclists who wanted a positive group ride experience, the club now hosts weekly group rides, training sessions, and seasonal events.

Club leadership

  • Ride Coordinator: Maya Chen

What we believe

Every rider deserves a friendly and inclusive environment. We promote safety, respect, and a passion for cycling.
